About Our Gym

Olympus Iron Gym is a state-of-the-art fitness facility located in the heart of Manhattan. Our gym spans 20,000 square feet and is equipped with the latest in fitness technology and equipment. Whether you're a beginner or an advanced athlete, we have everything you need to succeed.

Our mission is to provide an inclusive, motivating environment where every member can pursue their fitness goals. We believe in the power of community and hard work.
